Implemented (observed):
- Algorithm independent of HTTP/DB/provider: inputs are frozen eligible canonical candidates, concept weights,
  preferences, budget and config version; output is ordered selection decisions with marginal / coverage / quality
  gains, newly covered concepts and a grounded reason.
- Explicit objective: probabilistic weighted coverage + (β/k)·quality, monotone submodular; greedy recomputes
  conditional gains each step with stable tie-breaks; never exceeds budget or repeats; warns on small pools.
- Preferences (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced × Balanced/Foundational/Recent) adjust quality weights inside the same
  algorithm; accessibility is a weak, documented cue.
- Compared with all baselines on the committed datasets; sensitivity sweep and side-by-side lists generated offline.
