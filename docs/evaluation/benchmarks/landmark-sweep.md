# Landmark sweep (D16)

Two pre-declared mechanisms. (1) Landmark share λ = part of the influence weight given to log(1 + citations) / log(1 + pool max) instead of the normalized percentile, with quality share β. (2) Landmark seeds = up to that many reserved slots (at most max(1, k / 5)) for the pool's 5 most-cited works that have strong relevance evidence, placed before greedy selection; declared after (1) failed. Landmarks = how many of the pool's 5 most-cited works are kept. Loss = coverage of the unchanged setting (β = 4, λ = 0, no seeds) minus this setting's. D12 = coverage above the best baseline, relevance mean ≥ 0.95 and median percentile ≥ 0.9.

Pre-registered acceptance, judged on the audit topics only (AI-assisted medical diagnosis, graph neural networks, reinforcement learning in robotics): mean landmarks at k = 10 ≥ 2.0 with every topic ≥ 1; coverage loss ≤ 0.05 for every topic and budget; D12 still met. Choice = the first passing setting in the order below (smallest change first). Other topics are validation only.

**Chosen:** none — no setting met every criterion
